Privacy Policy

1. App Overview

PRIMEBLE is a mobile game that allows users to play, save progress, sign in with Google, create a public player profile, participate in rankings, and access a virtual store with optional in-app purchases.

2. Data We Collect

When you use PRIMEBLE, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Name: obtained from your Google account or player profile.
  • Nickname: chosen by the user for display in rankings.
  • Age: provided by the user during profile setup.
  • Country: selected by the user during profile setup.
  • Country flag: selected based on the chosen country and shown in rankings.
  • Email address: obtained from Google Sign-In for account identification.
  • Google user ID: used to identify the account securely.
  • Profile photo URL: if provided by the Google account.
  • Player icon: chosen by the user.
  • Game score: including best score and weekly ranking score.
  • Save data: stored locally on the device to resume game progress.
  • Purchase status: when in-app purchases are used, PRIMEBLE may store purchase entitlement or transaction status needed to unlock virtual items or features.

3. How We Use Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To allow users to sign in with Google.
  • To create and manage a player profile.
  • To save and display scores in weekly rankings.
  • To show nickname, player icon, country flag, and score in the ranking list.
  • To maintain game progress locally on the device.
  • To improve account continuity when users change or restore devices.
  • To manage virtual items, rewards, upgrades, or purchases made through Google Play Billing.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, or manipulation of rankings and purchases.

4. Public Ranking Information

PRIMEBLE may display public ranking information to other users. This may include: nickname, selected player icon, selected country flag, and score.

PRIMEBLE does not publicly display the user's email address, Google user ID, age, or private account details.

5. Data Sharing

PRIMEBLE does not sell personal data. Data may be processed using third-party services necessary for app functionality, including Google and Firebase services.

Public ranking information may be visible to other users, including nickname, selected icon, country flag, and score.

6. Third-Party Services

PRIMEBLE uses or may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: for user authentication.
  • Firebase Authentication: for account login and account identity.
  • Firebase Firestore: for storing user profile and ranking data.
  • Google Play Billing: for processing optional in-app purchases.

7. Payments and In-App Purchases

PRIMEBLE includes or may include a virtual store with optional in-app purchases. Purchases may include virtual items, upgrades, bonuses, or access to additional features.

Payments are processed through Google Play Billing. PRIMEBLE does not directly collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account information, or other financial payment credentials.

PRIMEBLE may store purchase status, entitlement information, or transaction references necessary to confirm and deliver purchased virtual items or features.

8. Data Storage and Security

User profile and ranking data are stored using Firebase Firestore. Access is protected using Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. Local save data is stored on the user's device.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Account profile data is retained while the user keeps an active PRIMEBLE account. Weekly ranking data may reset or rotate weekly. Local save data remains on the user's device unless the user clears app data or uninstalls the app.

Purchase-related entitlement data may be retained as needed to provide purchased items, restore purchases, prevent fraud, or comply with platform and legal requirements.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting: quantum.zenith.peru@gmail.com.

When a deletion request is received, we will delete associated user profile data and ranking records linked to the user's account, unless retention is required by applicable law, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or platform obligations.

11. Children and Age Information

PRIMEBLE asks for age to support basic user profile information and responsible account handling. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, they may contact us to request deletion.

12. International Users

Users may access PRIMEBLE from different countries. By using the app, users understand that their data may be processed through Firebase and Google services according to Google's infrastructure and applicable policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
